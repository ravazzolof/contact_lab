CLAB: the Contact LAB

We research contact improvisation:
define questions, experiments, scores,
observe, share, integrate and repeat

…

We listen to what is available:
our senses, limbs, organs, brains,
objects, colors, lights, instruments

…

We follow what questions alive in us.
Where does the movement come from?
How does intention change touch?

…

We welcome other absorption practices:
yoga, thai-chi, martial and visual arts,
poetry, jazz, dance, theater

…

We all are teachers and students,
share responsibility
and progress together

…

We are inquisitive, diverse bodies,
drawn to deep psychological, social,
and, why not, political transformation.

Does it resonate? Join us. Some experience is required, but dedication and curiosity are as important. Send an email to amsterdam.contact.lab@gmail.com
