CLAB: the Contact LAB

Amsterdam Contact Lab

A laboratory for research on contact improvisation.
Together we define questions, design experiments
and scores, observe, share, integrate and repeat

…

We research what is alive in us.
Where does the movement come from?
How does intention change breath?

…

We use all the tools available:
our senses, limbs, organs, brains,
but also objects, colors, lights, instruments

…

We welcome other practices of absorption:
meditation, thai-chi, yoga, martial and visual arts,
contemporary dance and theater, poetry, jazz

…

We are all both teachers and students,
share responsibility for each other
and progress together, as one

…

We are open, inquisitive, diverse bodies,
drawn to deep collective psychological, social,
and, why not, one day, political transformation.
